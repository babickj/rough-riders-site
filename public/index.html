      Admin

      Access requests

      People

      Admins manage people and settings. Coaches see player stats, player pages, video, coach notes, and the AI tools, and upload photos and video. Scorekeepers enter games and keep live score. Everyone else sees the schedule, scores, team stats, and team photos without an account.

      Add a person

      New accounts get a temporary password you can pass along. They can change it under My account. If the person already has an account from the other team, they keep their current password.

      Teams

      The web name becomes the team's address, for example . Use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes. You're automatically admin on every team.

      Spond schedule sync

      Games come over from Spond every two hours, or when you press Sync now on the Schedule tab. Practices and other non-game events are skipped. Name games in Spond like "vs Cardinals" or "@ Padres" so the opponent and home or away come through. Edits to a game's date, time, opponent, or field should be made in Spond, since the next sync copies Spond's version.

      TeamSnap or other calendar sync

      Where do I find the link?

      TeamSnap: sign in on a computer (the phone app doesn't show the link), open the team, go to Schedule, then Settings, then Sync Calendar / Export, and copy the calendar link.

      GameChanger, SportsEngine, TeamReach, LeagueApps: look for "Sync to calendar", "Subscribe", or "iCal" on the team schedule and copy that link.

      Google Calendar: calendar Settings, Integrate calendar, copy the "Secret address in iCal format".

      Games come over every two hours, or when you press Sync now on the Schedule tab. Practices are skipped, cancelled games show as cancelled, and games deleted in the other app come off this schedule. Edit a game's date, time, or field in the other app, since the next sync copies its version. The link is kept private on the server.
